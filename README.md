# The Wachtel Letters — Kraków, 1934–1939

A memorial website for twenty-four letters, postcards, and documents exchanged between a Jewish family in Kraków and their son Albert Wachtel in New York, written between 1934 and 1939 — the five years before the German invasion of Poland.

**Helena, Rosa, and Ida Wachtel were killed by the Nazis. These letters are what survived.**

---

## About This Site

This memorial website was built to preserve and share the Wachtel family correspondence. It presents each document with:

- **Original scan** — the Polish-language scan as a PDF
- **English translation** — full translation with translator's notes
- **Summary** — context about each letter and its place in the family's story

The site uses warm sepia and cream tones, careful typography, and a quiet, respectful design intended to honor the voices in these letters.

---

## How to Deploy on GitHub Pages

1. **Fork or clone this repository**

2. **Add the letter PDFs** to the `letters/` folder. Each PDF should be named exactly as listed in the site data (e.g., `brudley_120810_200419.pdf`). The PDF filenames in `index.html` match the original scan filenames.

3. **Go to your repository Settings → Pages**

4. **Under "Source," select your main branch and the root folder (`/`)**

5. **Click Save.** Your site will be live at `https://[your-username].github.io/[repo-name]/`

## Family

Albert Wachtel (b. 1907, Kraków) emigrated to New York in June 1936. His sisters Helena, Rosa, and Ida, his stepmother Sidonia Fingerhut, and his father Lable Wachtel were killed by the Nazis.

In later life, Albert wrote a brief archival note indexing this collection:

> *"Albert sisters — Helen, Rosa, Ida, brothers killed by Nazis. Letters from Albert's sisters in Krakow to him in U.S."*

These letters are what survived.
